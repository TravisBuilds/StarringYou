StarringYou - Image Credits
============================

Sample Images
-------------
The sample images in public/sample-images/ are placeholders.
Replace them with properly licensed images from:

- Unsplash (https://unsplash.com) - Unsplash License
- Pexels (https://www.pexels.com) - Pexels License

Format for attribution:
model-1.jpg - Photo by [Photographer Name] on [Platform]
URL: [original photo URL]
